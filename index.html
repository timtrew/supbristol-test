Brand colour reference

The names and hex values here are exactly as issued on the brand reference sheet.

  • SUP Blue (Primary Colour on the reference sheet)#28ADE4Header, primary buttons, links, availability heatmap
  • Bristol Sunflower#DCC63EPrivate groups theming: tags, tabs, panels
  • Bristol Maroon#A61E5BStar ratings, standard
  • Bristol Pink (Secondary Colour, Pink on the reference sheet)#DD87BCStar ratings on dark backgrounds, calendar nearly full days, spaces left badge
  • Bristol Green#87A554Calendar available days
  • Bristol Deep Sea#1E7383Not yet used anywhere on site
  • SUP Dark Grey (Secondary Colour, Dark Grey on the reference sheet; called “SUP Dark Grey” in the code and in comments, since this hex may change)#152B3BThe site's one dark ground: footer, every hero/photo scrim (home, activity pages, About and similar interior pages), the adventure and group-session card gradients, and the calendar's selected day. Always reached through var(--brand-slate), never a hardcoded rgb triple, so all of it moves together if this value changes.
  • SUP Light Grey (Secondary Colour, Grey Blue on the reference sheet)#9BAEBCCalendar unavailable days
  • SUP Light Grey, 10% (not on the reference sheet itself, but derived from it rather than an arbitrary value)color-mix(in srgb,var(--brand-grey) 10%,white)The site's page background (--paper), sitting behind every card, site-wide.
  • Mendip Dark Grey#3C3C3BNot yet used anywhere on site

Type scale

Headings are Rift Soft Bold (uppercase, the site's own licensed heading face); body text is Poppins.

  • Aa

    H1 | Rift Soft BoldPage titles: homepage hero, activity page name (a smaller H1 variant there shares the photo with price and description)

  • AaAa

    A title that wraps two linesLine height .92, not the usual 1.05: the gap between the two reads as one title rather than two stacked headlines when it comes out close to the width of a capital T's own stroke, not a fraction of the font's line-height. Used on the homepage hero.

  • Aa

    H2 | Rift Soft BoldSection headings: "Upcoming Availability", "Choose your adventure"

  • Aa

    H3 | Rift Soft BoldSub-section headings: "About this session", "Practical detail"

  • Aa

    H4 | Rift Soft BoldCard titles: product cards, "What's included", "Not suitable for"

  • Aa

    Body | PoppinsParagraph copy: session descriptions, FAQ answers

  • Aa

    Lede | PoppinsIntro strap line under a title: activity page standfirst

  • Aa

    Body small | PoppinsReviews ("4.9 from 171 reviews"), secondary meta text

  • Aa

    Tiny | PoppinsFine print: captions, small labels
